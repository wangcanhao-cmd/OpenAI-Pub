Why Mississippi?

The Hospitality State as a study destination.

The 2018 StudyMississippi profile highlighted a temperate climate, friendly communities, scenic beaches, musical heritage, cuisine, varied academic fields, and comparatively affordable study options. It also described Mississippi institutions in rural, suburban, and urban settings.

01

Academic range

The historic profile highlighted fields including agriculture, aerospace, engineering, management, international business, law, pharmacy, arts and sciences, veterinary and medical sciences, and nursing.

02

Culture & place

Mississippi's legacy StudyMississippi materials emphasized hospitality, music, food, beaches, and communities across the state.

03

Education & careers

The 2018 brochure described collaboration between companies and higher education institutions as part of the state's education and career landscape.

Historic descriptive content is based on the StudyMississippi profile in the 2018 NAFSA brochure and is presented as legacy material rather than current statistical claims.
