Discover Mississippi

Why study in Mississippi?

A distinctive place to learn, connect, and build a global perspective — from the Delta and central Mississippi to the Gulf Coast.

Academic opportunity

Many paths to a degree.

Mississippi includes public universities, independent colleges and universities, and a statewide community college system. Students can explore undergraduate, graduate, transfer, professional, and workforce pathways.

Community

Study with a sense of place.

Mississippi offers campus communities shaped by hospitality, regional culture, music, foodways, history, and landscapes that range from river communities to the Gulf Coast.

Research & careers

Connect study with experience.

Future versions of Study Mississippi will highlight research strengths, internships, career resources, and institutional partnerships across the state.

International support

Find the right campus resources.

Study Mississippi is being designed as a gateway to international offices, admissions information, immigration resources, campus life, and student-support contacts.
