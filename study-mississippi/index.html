Our association

Mississippi Association of International Educators

The Mississippi Association of International Educators (MAIE) works alongside Study Mississippi to support international education, institutional collaboration, and global engagement across the state.

MAIE supports Mississippi higher education professionals engaged in international education, recruitment, collaboration, student support, and campus internationalization.
