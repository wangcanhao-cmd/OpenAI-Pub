Legacy StudyMississippi profile

Established as a statewide consortium.

A 2018 NAFSA brochure described StudyMississippi as established in 2011 to connect international students and professionals with Mississippi education and training. It described members ranging from schools and community colleges to public/private universities and English-language programs.
